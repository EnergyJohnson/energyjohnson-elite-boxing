Who We Are

A development brand built around confidence, resilience and care.

Founded by Simon Johnson, Energy Johnson brings together elite coaching standards, youth mentorship and wellbeing education. Olympic-style boxing is one carefully structured tool within a wider mission: helping children, teenagers and adults move well, think positively and develop the habits that support healthy, confident lives.

“The goal is not simply to teach boxing. The goal is to help people leave every session standing taller.”

Why It Matters

Positive environments shape stronger futures.

Today’s young people benefit from purposeful spaces that encourage movement, leadership, teamwork and self-belief. Energy Johnson complements schools, families and communities with structured coaching experiences that promote respect, discipline, nutrition awareness and a positive mindset.

Every programme is designed to feel safe, aspirational and professionally delivered — creating memorable development experiences without losing warmth, energy or human connection.

Community Impact

A mission that travels beyond the studio, the school and the ring.

Through the Energy Johnson Foundation, Simon and his team completed a six-week community outreach programme in Colombia, delivering mentoring, Olympic-style boxing, fitness and educational support for children.

This work reflects the heart of the organisation: using movement, discipline and personal attention to create confidence, connection and opportunity. It is not a separate story from the brand — it is evidence of its values and long-term purpose.

Meet Simon

Founder, mentor and international performance coach.

Simon Johnson has spent more than 25 years coaching across the UK and UAE, working with youth athletes, elite performers, private clients and families. His experience includes professional boxing environments, international resort programming and long-term youth development.

As founder of Energy Johnson, Simon combines technical Olympic-style boxing knowledge with mentorship, lifestyle guidance and a calm, premium coaching presence. His work is rooted in helping people build confidence, resilience and healthier routines — at every age and ability level.
